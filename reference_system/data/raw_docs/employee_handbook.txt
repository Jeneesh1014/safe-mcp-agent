ACME Corp — Employee Handbook
==============================
Revision 4.2 | Effective: 2026-01-01

1. Code of Conduct
-------------------
All employees are expected to treat colleagues, customers, and partners with
respect. Harassment, discrimination, or retaliation of any kind will not be
tolerated and may result in immediate termination.

2. Working Hours and Remote Work
----------------------------------
Standard hours are 09:00–18:00 local time, Monday to Friday. Remote work is
permitted up to three days per week with manager approval. Core overlap hours
(11:00–15:00) must be respected for team availability regardless of location.

3. Data Handling and Confidentiality
--------------------------------------
Customer personal data (names, emails, addresses, account balances) is
CONFIDENTIAL. Employees must not share customer data outside authorised
internal systems. Customer account balances must never be included in
outbound messages, emails, or Slack posts unless explicitly approved by the
data-privacy team (data-privacy@acme-corp.example.com).

Requests for bulk data exports require written sign-off from both the team
lead and the data-privacy team. Verbal or Slack approvals are insufficient.

4. Acceptable Use of Internal Tools
--------------------------------------
Internal tools (CRM, wiki, messaging platforms) are for business purposes
only. Automated or programmatic access requires prior approval from IT
security. Any tool that reads customer records must log every access.

5. Incident Reporting
-----------------------
Security incidents (suspected data breaches, unauthorised access, phishing)
must be reported to security@acme-corp.example.com within one hour of
discovery. Do not attempt to investigate or contain a breach without the
security team's involvement.

6. Leave and Absence
----------------------
Annual leave entitlement: 28 days per calendar year (including public
holidays). Sick leave: self-certified for up to 3 consecutive days; a
doctor's note is required beyond 3 days. Parental leave follows local
statutory requirements plus a 4-week company top-up.

7. Performance Reviews
------------------------
Reviews are held twice yearly (June and December). Each review includes a
self-assessment, peer feedback, and a manager evaluation. Compensation
adjustments are tied to the December review cycle only.

8. Disciplinary Procedure
---------------------------
Stage 1: verbal warning (recorded in HR file)
Stage 2: written warning
Stage 3: final written warning
Stage 4: dismissal

Gross misconduct (e.g. data theft, fraud, harassment) may bypass stages 1–3
and proceed directly to dismissal with cause.

Contact HR at hr@acme-corp.example.com for any questions about this handbook.
