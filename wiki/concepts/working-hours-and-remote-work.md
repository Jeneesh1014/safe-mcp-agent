# Working Hours and Remote Work

Standard working hours are 09:00–18:00 local time, Monday to Friday. Remote work is permitted up to three days per week with manager approval. Core overlap hours (11:00–15:00) must be respected for team availability regardless of location.

This policy ensures that teams can maintain a healthy balance between work and personal life while also ensuring that critical core hours are covered.

See [[summaries/employee_handbook]] for more information on company policies regarding working hours, remote work, and data confidentiality.
