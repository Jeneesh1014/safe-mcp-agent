# Access Control

*Access to systems is granted on a least-privilege basis.*

## Types of Access

*   **User Access**: Employees have access to systems based on their role. The access level is based on the job description and department.

*   **Shared Credentials**: Shared credentials are prohibited, and each user must have an individual account.

*   **Service Accounts**: Service accounts require IT security approval and must be reviewed quarterly.

## Key Considerations

*   *Role-Based Access Control*: Employees may only access data and systems required for their current role.

*   *Least Privilege Principle*: The principle of assigning the least privileges to employees to reduce the risk of unauthorized access.

*   *Access Revocation*: Access is revoked within 24 hours of role change or employment termination.
