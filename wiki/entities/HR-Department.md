# Human Resources
HR-Department is responsible for all matters related to employees.
They oversee employee benefits, handle disciplinary actions, and provide support for company policies.
Contact HR at hr@acme-corp.example.com for any questions or concerns about the Employee Handbook.
## Key Facts
The HR department is a key part of ACME Corp's operations. They work closely with other departments to ensure employees are happy and productive.
HR also handles employee complaints and grievances, and provides training on company policies and procedures.
