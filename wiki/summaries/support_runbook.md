## ACME Corp — Customer Support Runbook Summary

This runbook outlines procedures for handling customer enquiries, account issues, billing disputes, and escalations. It emphasizes verifying customer identity, following established dispute windows for invoices, and adhering to information security policies.

### Key Concepts
- Identity verification: Verifying customer identity through registered email address and last four characters of account ID.
- Billing disputes: Handling disputes within accepted time frames and referring cases to the finance team when necessary.
- Account balance queries: Confirming account balances without stating exact figures, directing customers to self-service portals instead.

### Findings and Ideas
- Standard procedures must be followed in order; skipping sections is not acceptable.
- Security concerns are to be reported immediately, particularly for automated requests.
- End-of-shift checklists ensure all open tickets have a status update and no sensitive data is left unsaved.
